Common mistakes

Six shortcuts that create longer detours.

The safest beginner habit is simple: separate live evidence from confident-sounding guesses. These checks help you do that quickly.

01

Waiting for a magic wall number

No current wall thresholds are confirmed here. Read the live requirement and test progress instead.

02

Treating a strategy as an official rule

A useful route can still be player advice. Look for the Verified fact label before repeating a claim as game behavior.

03

Assuming rebirth resets specific items

Rebirth is verified, but its cost, multiplier, and reset behavior are not. Read the live confirmation screen.

04

Copying pet tables from another game

Pets give boosts, but this game's pet odds, rarities, and values are not confirmed by our sources.

05

Planning around unverified rewards

Food pickups, auto-click, offline earnings, daily rewards, and Wins spending are not confirmed here.

06

Mixing up similarly named games

+1 Fat to Break Walls is a different title. Its maps, values, and mechanics are not evidence for this game.

Guide strategy

If a tip has no source, use it as an experiment rather than a promise. Check one thing in-game, observe the result, and update your plan.

Check in-game
