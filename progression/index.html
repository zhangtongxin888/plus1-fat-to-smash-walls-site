Progression without fake numbers

Build a route around what is actually confirmed.

The game has a clear strength-and-smash loop. The precise economy is live data, so this guide focuses on sound decisions rather than fragile thresholds.

The progression map

Click→Fat→Stronger smash→Walls→Worlds & Wins
Verified fact

Pets provide boosts and rebirth makes you stronger. The official description does not give the exact values or explain the live economy.

What each system can safely tell us

Fat

Confirmed: Gained by clicking; bigger Fat is tied to stronger smashes.

Not confirmed: Exact gain rates and multipliers are not confirmed.

Walls & worlds

Confirmed: Breaking walls unlocks new worlds.

Not confirmed: Wall requirements and world details are not confirmed.

Wins

Confirmed: Wins can be collected, and the official description connects them to leaderboards.

Not confirmed: How Wins may be spent is not confirmed.

Pets

Confirmed: Pets can be hatched for boosts.

Not confirmed: Rarities, odds, costs, and boost values are not confirmed.

Rebirth

Confirmed: Rebirth is described as making you stronger.

Not confirmed: Cost, multiplier, and reset behavior are not confirmed.

A safe upgrade decision

  1. Identify the blocker. Are you trying to gain Fat faster, smash the next wall, or improve a longer-term boost?
  2. Read the live offer. Record the shown cost, effect, and any warning before acting.
  3. Compare one change. Change a single system so the result is easier to understand.
  4. Re-check after updates. Treat old screenshots and uncited tables as historical, not current truth.
Guide strategy

This four-step check is our decision framework, not an official mechanic. It is designed to keep your plan useful when balance details change.

Check in-game

The public product interface lists VIP, Golden Zone, Demon Zone, and Hacker Zone. Their effects are not confirmed, so this guide does not recommend or describe them.
